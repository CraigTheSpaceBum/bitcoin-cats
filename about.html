Building Sifaka.Live

Sifaka.Live is a Nostr-powered social and live-streaming client built to bring the Nostr experience together in one place. It lets people discover creators, watch and chat around live streams, and connect with others while keeping the open Nostr network at its core.

Features include live streaming with theater mode and chat, Nostr profiles and feeds, videos, direct messages, community Nest rooms, Podcast Radio, Bitcoin Lightning and on-chain support, and games such as chess.
